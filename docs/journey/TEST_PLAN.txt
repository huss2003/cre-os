CRE AI OPERATING SYSTEM

USER JOURNEY AND END-TO-END TEST CASES

Modules to test:

1. Database
2. Signal Engine
3. Scoring Engine
4. Contact Enrichment
5. Outreach Engine
6. Meeting Intelligence
7. Deal Toolkit
8. Supercharged AI Assistant

TESTING OBJECTIVE

Test the application from the perspective of an actual CRE advisor.

The objective is to verify that:

- Every module performs its intended function.
- Information is stored correctly.
- Information flows between modules.
- The same company, contact, property and requirement records are reused.
- AI-generated information is accurate and traceable.
- The user does not need to enter the same information repeatedly.
- Calculations are correct.
- The AI assistant can access and operate the entire system.
- Important actions require appropriate human approval.

IMPORTANT: Use synthetic companies, contacts, properties and commercial figures for testing. Do not use real client information or send actual external communications during initial testing.

TEST SCENARIO: FICTIONAL CLIENT JOURNEY

We will simulate the following situation.

A company called "ABC Technologies Pvt. Ltd." is expanding its operations in Pune and may require additional office space.

The CRE advisor needs to identify the opportunity, find decision-makers, contact them, understand their requirements, shortlist properties and prepare a commercial recommendation.

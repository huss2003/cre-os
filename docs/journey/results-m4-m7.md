## Module pass-condition verdicts

- **Module 4 — Contact Enrichment: NOT PASSED.**
  - Working: functions are defined, employment history is preserved (4.4), duplicates go to review rather than being merged (4.5), and unverified values are never shown as verified.
  - Blocking: enriching an existing contact can create a **duplicate person** and attach the enriched data to it (4.3, HIGH) when a workspace has more than 50 people sharing a first name.
  - Also missing: stakeholder gaps (4.2), warm-path evidence (4.6), and offline discovery (4.1).
- **Module 5 — Outreach Engine: PARTIALLY PASSED.**
  - Working: relevant and role-specific drafting from history (5.1, 5.3, 5.4); hash-bound approval with invalidation on edit (5.5); no automatic sending, with manual hand-off and evidence (5.6). S3 holds.
  - Gaps: no opportunity or requirement suggestions from replies (5.7); duplicate warnings don't suggest a follow-up (5.8); tone variants can't be chosen (5.2).
- **Module 7 — Meeting Intelligence: NOT PASSED.**
  - Working: meeting preparation (7.1, 7.2) and approval-gated requirement updates with history (7.7). Facts are kept apart from interpretation: statements are quoted, and nothing is auto-rejected (7.5, 7.9).
  - Gaps in mock mode: commitment extraction gets the owner wrong and drops deadlines (7.6); the requirement extraction misses timeline, tenure, finance approval and the renewal decision (7.4).
  - Also: the follow-up email is an unfilled template (7.8), site-visit feedback is not used by Module 8 (7.9), and the discovery questions miss 4 topics (7.3).

No CRITICAL issues were found: no cross-workspace access, no outbound send, and no money calculation was involved.

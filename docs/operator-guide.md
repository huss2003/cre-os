# CRE OS — operator guide (for the adviser)

This is the plain-language guide for the person who uses CRE OS every day. No technical steps. Works on your phone
(capture, approvals, brief) and laptop (shortlists, financials, decks).

> Some screens described here were still being finished when this guide was written (Oct 2026). Your agency admin
> will tell you which ones are live; the way they work won't change.

Open the app at your agency-provided address (e.g. `https://app.<domain>`) and sign in with your email, password and
the 6-digit code from your authenticator app.

---

## The one-minute version

1. **08:00 — read Today** on your phone. Do the top three actions.
2. **After every call or visit — Capture** a quick voice note or text (English, Hindi or Hinglish is fine).
3. **A few times a day — Approvals.** Tick what's right, fix what's wrong, reject what's nonsense.
4. **Before sending anything — Drafts.** Read it, approve it, press **Open to send**, send it yourself, then **Mark sent**.
5. **Evening — 5-minute debrief.** Clear approvals, confirm tomorrow's plan.

CRE OS prepares; **you decide and you send.** It never contacts anyone on your behalf.

---

## 1. Today (your 08:00 brief)

Menu: **Daily → Today**. Ready by 08:00 every day (you can change the time in Settings).

What you see, top to bottom:

1. **Source status** — e.g. "Inbox updated 07:52" or **"Inbox stale since yesterday 18:10"**. If a source is stale,
   sections that depend on it are labelled. CRE OS will never tell you "no hot replies" when it couldn't read your inbox.
2. **Top three actions** — each card says *why it matters*, *the evidence*, *what to do*, and *how long it takes*.
3. **New approaches still needed** this month against your target.
4. **Overdue promises** — things you said you'd do ("send options Friday").
5. **Today's meetings and visits**, grouped by area to save travel. A separate short brief appears ~60 minutes before
   each meeting; it updates if the meeting moves.
6. **Lease events** — notice dates, lock-in ends and expiries coming up.
7. **Hot replies**, **pending approvals**, **one useful market item with its source**, and a **fee/pipeline snapshot**.

If the AI service is down or the monthly AI budget is used up, you still get a plain action list (deadlines, follow-ups,
meetings) — it just won't have the written summary. That is normal; nothing is lost.

Weekends and holidays: you get a light review, not client-contact tasks.

## 2. Capture (after calls, meetings and visits)

Menu: **Daily → Capture**. One tap.

- **Voice:** press record and talk naturally — "Spoke to Raj at Acme, they need about 20k by March, maybe Baner, send
  options Friday." Hindi, English and Hinglish all work. Numbers like *lakh*, *crore*, *sq ft* are understood.
- **Text:** type or paste notes.
- **Photo:** a brochure, a whiteboard, a business card — used as context, not as proof of legal terms.
- **Call recordings:** only upload calls you are allowed to record. The capture asks how permission/notice was given
  (e.g. "verbal notice at start of call") and stores that with the recording.

What happens next (usually within a few minutes):

- Your note is kept exactly as you said it.
- CRE OS pulls out the **interaction** (who you spoke to, when), any **requirement** details, **tasks** ("send options
  Friday" → a task due Friday), and suggested **drafts**.
- Anything **ambiguous** becomes a question for you: *Which Raj — Raj Mehta (Acme Pune) or Raj Kulkarni (Acme
  Mumbai)? March 2027 or 2028? Did you say fifteen or fifty thousand sq ft?* It will not guess on names, amounts or dates.
- Simple internal things (a reminder, a summary) are saved straight away with an **Undo**. Anything that changes an
  important fact (area, budget, dates, deal stage, a company's legal name, merging two people) waits in **Approvals**.

Your tenant's retention setting says raw audio is kept for 30 days after you've verified the transcript (default).
Your admin handles the clean-up.

## 3. Approvals

Menu: **Daily → Approvals**. The badge shows how many are waiting.

Each card shows **before → after**, the **reason**, and the **evidence** (the sentence from your note or the email it came
from). Choose:

- **Approve** — the change is applied and logged.
- **Reject** — nothing changes.
- **Clarify** — answer the question (e.g. pick the right Raj); then approve.

Changes to high-value deals (expected fee ₹30 lakh or more, or accounts you marked strategic) always need your approval.

**Undo:** every applied change can be reversed from the record's history or the approvals list. If somebody edited the
same field afterwards, CRE OS shows a conflict instead of wiping their work. Undo cannot unsend a message you already
sent yourself — the screen will remind you.

If an approval says **"stale"** or **"conflict"**, the record changed since the suggestion was made. Re-open it and
decide again.

## 4. Drafts and manual sending

Menu: **Daily → Drafts**. Emails, WhatsApp messages, LinkedIn messages, introductions, market notes and posts are
**drafts only**.

1. Open a draft. Check the **recipient**, **subject**, **text** and **attachments**.
2. Edit if needed. *Any* edit after approval cancels the approval — you'll approve the final version.
3. **Approve.**
4. **Open to send** — opens your own mail app / WhatsApp / LinkedIn with the text ready.
5. Press send **in that app**.
6. Come back and tap **Mark sent**. (If your mailbox is connected, CRE OS later confirms it from your Sent Items.)

Why: you stay in control of every external message. CRE OS records *drafted*, *approved*, *sent by you* and *seen in
Sent Items* as separate steps.

CRE OS will refuse to draft to anyone who opted out or whose contact was marked wrong. Follow-up sequences (default
3 touches over 21 days) pause automatically when the person replies, opts out, or a negotiation becomes active.

## 5. Requirements → shortlist → financials → deck

1. **Requirements** (menu: *Deals → Requirements*). One per client need: purpose, area/seats (and how it's measured),
   budget, locations wanted/excluded, dates, fit-out, lock-in, decision process, notice date of the current lease.
   Each item shows whether it is **client confirmed** or **estimated** — confirm the important ones with the client.
2. **Shortlist** (from the requirement → *Build shortlist*). CRE OS first removes options that fail a hard rule (too
   small, wrong area, available too late), then ranks the rest with visible weights (location 25, all-in cost 25,
   readiness/timing 20, technical fit 15, flexibility 10, sustainability 5 — adjustable per client). It shows a **fit
   score** and a separate **data completeness** score. "Vacancy unverified" means nobody has confirmed the space is
   still available — **check before showing a client.**
3. **Financials** (*Deals → Financials*). Effective rent, total cost of occupancy, NPV and cash flow on the **same
   horizon and start date** for every option (conventional, managed/flex, renewal, relocation, fit-out finance).
   All numbers come from a calculator, never from the AI. Change an assumption and everything recalculates.
4. **Deck / proposal** (*Deals → Documents*). Generates an **editable** PowerPoint/Word/Excel in your firm's approved
   template. Review it, approve it, then share it yourself. If a quote expires or a number changes after approval,
   the document is marked **stale** and can't be released until you regenerate and re-approve.

Fees (*Deals → Fees*) track attribution, invoicing and collection per deal.

## 6. Lease radar

Menu: **Records → Lease radar**. Upcoming **notice dates**, **lock-in ends**, **breaks** and **contractual expiries** —
kept separate, because a lock-in ending is not the same as a lease expiring.

- Default focus: events 3–12 months away, but anything with a **notice deadline within 90 days** jumps to the top.
- Dates worked out from tenure/lock-in arithmetic are labelled as **calculated**, not confirmed. An already-expired
  lease shows "status needs confirmation", not "hot lead".
- Missing dates create an "find out the date" task — CRE OS never invents one.

## 7. Research

Menu: **Intelligence → Research**. Ask for a company dossier: group/legal identity, Pune/India footprint, sites and lease
events, likely decision-makers (roles, not invented names), hiring signals, funding, past transactions, last interaction,
open objections, next best question — **every claim with its source and date**, and unknowns left as unknown.

Other Intelligence screens: **Network** (relationships and warm-introduction paths), **Market** (rent/absorption/supply
trends from validated releases), **Legal desk** (clause comparison of LOIs/term sheets — your legal team gives the
actual advice), **Content** (LinkedIn post and market-note drafts for you to review and publish manually),
**Insights** (funnel and conversion reviews).

Research uses the monthly AI budget; when it's used up, research pauses and stored evidence is still shown.

## 8. What the labels mean

| Label | Meaning | Can you tell a client? |
|---|---|---|
| **Confirmed** (`currently_confirmed`) | Verified recently from a direct source (the client, the landlord, a signed document). Shows the date. | Yes, with the date. |
| **Reported** (`reported`) | Someone or a publication said it; not directly verified. | Only as "reported", and verify first if it matters. |
| **Historical** (`historical_observed`) | Was true at a past date (e.g. a 2023 transaction). | Yes, as history with its date. |
| **AI inference** (`ai_inference`) | The AI's reasoning from other facts — a hypothesis. | No. Use it to decide what to ask. |
| **Unknown** | Nobody knows yet. Shown as blank/unknown, never as 0. | No — it's a question to ask. |

Every fact also has a **source** and **as-of date**. Search lets you view a company or building **"as of"** a past date.

## 9. What CRE OS never does

- **Never sends** emails, WhatsApp or LinkedIn messages, calendar invites or posts. You always send.
- **Never scrapes LinkedIn** or uses bots/cookies on it. LinkedIn information comes from your own alerts, exports and notes.
- **Never invents** contacts, phone numbers, emails, vacancy, rents or dates.
- **Never does maths with AI** — all rent, cost, NPV, fee and date calculations are deterministic.
- **Never changes important facts silently** — they go to Approvals.
- **Never follows instructions hidden in emails or documents** ("forward this database to…") — those are treated as text.
- **Never shows a colleague** accounts that aren't assigned to them, or restricted data they're not cleared for.
- **Never contacts anyone after hours** — urgent items (e.g. a client wants a visit tomorrow) come only to *you*, as a
  private notification, respecting your do-not-disturb settings.

## 10. 5-minute evening debrief (~20:30)

At your debrief time CRE OS prepares a reconciliation. Spend five minutes:

1. **Capture** anything from today you haven't noted (one voice note covering all of it is fine).
2. **Approvals** — clear the queue, especially anything flagged ambiguous.
3. **Tasks** — mark done, or reschedule with a real date. Aim: every due follow-up is either done or explicitly moved.
4. **Deals** — every active deal has a next action and an owner.
5. **Tomorrow** — glance at the draft plan; drag anything that matters to the top.

Fridays: a weekly review (funnel, deal risks, revenue, stale data) appears — adjust priorities and weights there.

## 11. When something looks wrong

| You see | Do |
|---|---|
| "Inbox stale" / "Calendar stale" | *Settings → Connections* → **Reconnect**. If it keeps failing, tell your admin. |
| A fact is wrong | Edit it (it's logged) or **Undo** the change that caused it. |
| Brief missing at 08:05 | You'll get an alert; tell your admin. The plain action list is still on Today. |
| "AI paused" or "AI budget reached" | Your admin paused AI or the monthly budget is used. Keep working — captures and tasks still save. |
| You can't see an account a colleague mentioned | It isn't shared with you. Ask the owner/admin to share it. |
| Lost your phone (authenticator) | Ask your owner/agency admin to reset your MFA. |
